Runbook 12: Post-Recovery Reconciliation

Scope

This runbook covers validation and reconciliation after a disaster recovery event.

Objective

  • Confirm data integrity after failover or recovery
  • Reconcile transaction volumes and settlement records

Preconditions

  • Reconciliation processes exist for payments, settlements, and merchant balances.
  • Audit logs and transaction event stores are available.

Immediate Actions

  1. Collect transaction data from both regions and payment systems.
  2. Compare counts and values for all processed transactions.
  3. Validate no duplicate or missing transactions occurred during recovery.

RTO Steps

  1. Reconcile payment event streams with backend settlement ledgers.
  2. Validate merchant account balances and reconciliation records.
  3. Fix any detected discrepancies with controlled rollback procedures.

Validation

  • Confirm transaction counts match expected volumes.
  • Verify settlement batches are complete.
  • Ensure audit logs show no gaps or unauthorized changes.

Post-Recovery Activities

  • Document reconciliation results and any corrections.
  • Review root cause and improve the DR process.
  • Share findings with the Board and compliance teams.
