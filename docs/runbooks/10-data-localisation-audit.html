Runbook 10: Data Localisation Audit

Scope

This runbook covers auditing and verifying that payment data remains within Indian AWS regions.

Objective

  • Ensure all regulated payment data is stored and processed only in Mumbai and Hyderabad
  • Demonstrate compliance with RBI and data localisation requirements

Preconditions

  • AWS Config rules are enabled for resource location.
  • Data classification and tagging are enforced.
  • Periodic inventory of storage resources exists.

Immediate Actions

  1. Run AWS Config conformance packs for regional compliance.
  2. Audit S3 bucket locations, DynamoDB tables, Aurora clusters, and Kafka topics.
  3. Confirm no snapshots or backups are stored outside India.

RTO Steps

  1. Remediate any out-of-compliance resources by relocating or deleting them.
  2. Re-run compliance checks.
  3. Document findings and remediation steps.

Validation

  • Confirm all resources are within ap-south-1 or ap-south-2.
  • Verify backups and snapshots are also localized.
  • Report compliance findings to the security team.

Post-Recovery Activities

  • Schedule regular audits.
  • Update policy enforcement for new service deployments.
  • Maintain evidence for inspection.
